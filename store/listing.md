**Full description** (4,000):

```
Buying land or a house in Nigeria? Check it first.

PropertyIntel by Capstone puts property due diligence in your pocket. Tell us which property you are considering, and our team searches the relevant public and government records for litigation, encumbrances and title discrepancies. Then they send you a clear report before you commit your money.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Search a property: enter the state, local government, address and any documents you have, such as a survey plan or deed.
2. Pay securely: settle your invoice in the app through Paystack.
3. Track every step: each search gets a tracking ID. Follow it from Submitted through Validated, Assigned and Under review to Closed.
4. Get your report: open your due-diligence report and documents right in the app.

WHAT YOU GET
• Searches against relevant land registry, court and government records
• Checks for registered interests, encumbrances and litigation
• Discrepancies flagged between your documents and official records
• A written report that explains the findings and recommends next steps

BUILT AROUND YOUR CASES
• An overview of your searches: total, in progress, reports ready and anything that needs your input
• Look up any case instantly with its tracking ID
• Recent searches kept on hand
• Raise a dispute on a closed case if something doesn't look right

WHO IT'S FOR
First-time buyers, diaspora Nigerians buying from abroad, investors, developers and anyone who wants to know what they are paying for before they pay.

A due-diligence report reflects the records available on the search date and is not a guarantee of title. See our Terms for the full scope of the service.

Questions? info@capstoneglobalhq.com
```
